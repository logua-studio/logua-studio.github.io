Logua Studio

Site photos that know where they were taken.

FootPoint is a field tool for locating, photographing and organizing engineering records. It captures position, direction and time as you shoot, lets you fill in zone, trade and stage with two taps on site, and turns the result into a PDF report or data files you can hand over.

Download on the App Store Get support

Organized by project and point

Work data stays out of your personal photo library. Each project carries its own quick-pick fields — location, zone number, position, trade, construction stage, shot type — so you tap instead of retyping the same thing on every photo.

Where it was taken, and which way

When you capture a photo in the app, its position and camera direction can be recorded with the shot, so someone who wasn't there can still tell which way the camera was facing. Positions are shown in WGS84, TWD97 and TWD67 together, each with its own accuracy.

Review on the map

Points, photos and shooting directions can all be reviewed on the map, so a handover doesn't depend on anyone's memory of the site.

Deliverables

Four PDF report templates — Complete, Concise, Measurement and Site Inspection — with a customizable layout and your company logo. Data exports as CSV, GeoJSON, KML, KMZ and TXT for an existing CAD or GIS workflow, plus photo packages and full archives.

Stays on your device

No separate account. Your points and photos stay on your device; to use them on more than one device, turn on iCloud sync and they're kept in your own iCloud account. Syncing, exporting and sharing are all your call.

English and Traditional Chinese

The interface follows your iPhone's language setting, and PDF reports are produced in the app's current language.
