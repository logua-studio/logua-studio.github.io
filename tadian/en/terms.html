What the service is for

踏點 is a tool for recording field coordinates, organizing photos and exporting data. It helps users keep point information through site inspection, patrol and data organization workflows in Taiwan.

Not a precision survey instrument

踏點 is not a cadastral survey, engineering survey or legal boundary determination tool. The coordinates, directions, accuracy values and conversion results shown in the app are for field records and data organization only.

User responsibilities

  • Decisions that matter for engineering, cadastral, legal or safety purposes should be confirmed by qualified professionals using professional instruments.
  • Stay aware of your surroundings in the field. Do not let operating the app distract you from traffic, terrain, weather or site hazards.
  • Before exporting or sharing data that contains locations and photos, make sure you are entitled to handle and share that content.
